About

In this ``reading group'', we (the organizers) will give a self-contained introduction to the theory behind diffusion models, loosely following the text A Mathematical Introduction to Diffusion Models. We will introduce the necessary mathematical background as we go (e.g. stochastic processes and PDEs), but will emphasize intuition and algorithmic concepts over full rigor. Later sessions will cover (a selection of) recent developments, including discrete diffusion models and inference-time steering.

The meeting time will be set by a poll sent to everyone who signs up.
